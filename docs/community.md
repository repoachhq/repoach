# The weekly "first ten users" ritual

Repoach has no users to point to yet other than its own maintainer
and Yoob — `SP-COMMUNITY-ONBOARDING`'s own `Gaps` section says so.
This ritual is how that changes: ten people, each with at least one
review run on their own repository, tracked one at a time rather than
chased as an aggregate growth number.

## Every Monday

- [ ] List who installed or tried Repoach this week — stars, new issues, new Discussions threads, PyPI downloads, Codespaces opens on the public demo — in a pinned GitHub Discussion thread.
- [ ] Contact each new user once (a reply on their issue, a comment on their star — not a cold DM).
- [ ] For anyone who tried Repoach and stalled, open an issue naming what blocked them, linked from the pinned thread.
- [ ] Update the counter at the top of the pinned thread: `users with ≥ 1 review on their own repository: N / 10`.
- [ ] TODO(owner): the time slot committed to this ritual every Monday (`SP-COMMUNITY-ONBOARDING` D3; proposed: one hour).

## When it stops

The ritual stops being a standing weekly item once the counter reads
`10 / 10` — at that point there are enough real users that the next
instrument is a survey or a changelog, not a one-by-one chase.
